Route notes

Lapp and Shira have multi-step questlines in The Ringed City. Shira's rewards depend on dialogue and later progression. For Lapp, discover the Purging Monument before completing his final sequence.

The Painted World has fewer conventional NPC questlines; check the Millwood/Crow settlements, the invading Livid Pyromancer Dunnel, and the Friede/Ariandel boss sequence.

DLC quest route reminders

Lapp — The Ringed City
Speak to Lapp at the collapsed tower, then at Earthen Peak Ruins. Find the Titanite Slab or report its location, speak to him near the Ringed Inner Wall, discover the Purging Monument, and return to him. Continue through the Shared Grave sequence to obtain his armor set. Avoid advancing blindly past each encounter.
Shira — The Ringed City
Speak to Shira near the Ringed Inner Wall and answer her question as prompted; exhaust dialogue to receive the Sacred Chime of Filianore and her request concerning Darkeater Midir. Her later appearance and reward depend on DLC progression.
Ariandel — Friede and the Painter
Explore the Painted World before committing to the final boss sequence. Exhaust Friede's dialogue, locate the route into the depths of the painting, defeat the DLC boss, and return to the Painter after the ending to complete the area's narrative interaction.
