Route conflicts & important decisions

Sirris vs. Rosaria

Joining Rosaria's Fingers or offering a Pale Tongue for appearance/stat changes can antagonize Sirris. Finish Sirris's questline and collect her rewards before committing to Rosaria's progression.

Yoel, Yuria & Usurpation of Fire

For this ending, obtain all five Dark Sigils from Yoel before entering the Catacombs of Carthus. Do not heal the Dark Sigils before the ending sequence.

Anri's routes

Anri's quest has alternate outcomes. Decide whether you want the Usurpation route or the standard quest outcome before following the Church of Yorshka and pilgrim-related steps.

Irina's miracles

Giving Irina dark tomes and purchasing dark miracles changes her quest outcome. For the safer good route, give her the non-dark tomes and have dark tomes handled by Karla.

Greirat's pillaging

Greirat's later expeditions depend on quest progression and protection. Check Siegward/Patches conditions before advancing the Irithyll and Lothric Castle stages.

Orbeck

Recruit Orbeck with sufficient Intelligence, give him scrolls as you find them, and purchase spells before progressing too far. His quest can affect rewards and summon availability.

Mound-Makers

Try to obtain the covenant through the cage-bearer before defeating the Curse-rotted Greatwood. If missed, the alternate Sirris route may still be available.

Ending choice

Keep separate saves or make a deliberate choice before the final sequence if you want multiple endings. The checklist records requirements, but does not replace a route-specific walkthrough.
